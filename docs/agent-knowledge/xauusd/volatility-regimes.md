## Rule

Read ATR as a regime, not a constant. Compression — ATR falling, narrow ranges — precedes expansion. Expansion — ATR rising, wide bars — mean-reverts. Match the timeframe and the stop to the regime you are in.

## Why

A 1.5 ATR stop is generous in a quiet market and tight in a violent one. The same signal rule produces different trade frequencies in each regime, and that is expected, not a bug.

## In practice

After a high-impact release, wait for ATR to settle before trusting a mean-reversion entry. In compression, expect false breaks at the range edges. In expansion, reduce size rather than widen the stop.
