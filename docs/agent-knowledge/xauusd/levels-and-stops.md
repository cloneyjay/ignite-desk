## Rule

Gold respects round numbers, the prior-day high and low, and the session extremes. Stops cluster just beyond those levels. Place the stop where the idea is wrong, not where the crowd's stop is.

## Why

Liquidity sits where stops sit. A stop a few cents beyond a round figure is the market's favourite target. A stop beyond the level that invalidates the setup survives the sweep and still keeps risk inside the plan.

## In practice

Mark the prior-day range and the current session high and low before reading the signal. If the planned stop sits inside an obvious cluster, the setup is poorer than the R:R suggests. Do not move the stop to avoid the cluster; skip the trade.
