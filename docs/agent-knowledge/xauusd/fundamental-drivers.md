## Rule

Gold is priced off real yields first, the dollar second, and fear third. Rising real yields raise the opportunity cost of a non-yielding asset and usually press gold down; falling real yields do the opposite. The dollar matters because gold is priced in it.

## Why

A forecast model sees price, not the reason behind it. Two identical M15 patterns can have opposite follow-through if one sits before a central-bank decision and the other after a quiet inflation print. Fundamentals explain the regime the pattern lives in.

## In practice

Check the calendar for central-bank decisions, CPI and payrolls before trusting a directional signal. Central-bank buying, ETF flows and geopolitics can override the yield relationship for weeks. Treat correlations as regime-dependent, never as laws.
