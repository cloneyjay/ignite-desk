## Rule

Gold trades nearly around the clock, but it does not trade the same way around the clock. The Asian session is thinner and range-bound; London brings the first real expansion; the London–New York overlap carries the deepest liquidity and the cleanest follow-through.

## Why

A breakout that works in the overlap often fails in the Asian lull. Spread, tick frequency and the probability of a false break all change with the session. The same M15 bar means different things at 03:00 and 15:00.

## In practice

Prefer the London open and the overlap for M5–M15 entries. Treat the last hour before the daily rollover as hostile: spreads widen and stops are picked off. Skip the first minutes of a session until the opening range prints.
